ORBITIQ

Orbit + IQ. Six specialist agents operating around one intelligent market command centre. The global network of markets, assets, and opportunities the platform observes — coordinated by intelligence that analyses information, identifies patterns, and makes decisions. All in paper mode. No live orders. Just intelligence.

The Mission

ORBITIQ is designed to give traders a single, intelligent interface to monitor multiple markets, evaluate risk, and simulate strategies without ever risking real capital. Every trade is paper. Every decision is evidence-based. Every risk is measured. The platform brings together real-time market data, AI-driven analysis, and multi-agent coordination into one command centre.

Security & Paper Trading

  • Paper-only: No real orders are ever placed. All trading is simulated.
  • Public data only: Market feeds use public exchange APIs (Bybit, Binance, Coinbase).
  • Hard risk limits: Daily loss limits, leverage caps, and position limits are enforced.
  • Private account monitor: Read-only — never touches your exchange credentials.
  • Copy trading: Discovers public Hyperliquid wallets and simulates their fresh fills.
  • Authentication: Secure email/password with SQLite-backed user database.
